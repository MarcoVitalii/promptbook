# How to send the script and your notes to the cast

Give the actors the current text on their phones, with the director's notes on the lines they concern, and do it again after each rehearsal without anyone losing their own notes.

## Once: make a department for notes

1. **☰** → **Send to cast…**
2. If it says no department is marked "Cast", press **Add a “Notes” department**.

Or, to choose its name: **☰** → **Settings…**, **+ Department**, give it a code (`DIR`) and a name (`Director`), and tick **Cast**. The name is what the actors read as the author of the note. You can have several: Director, Choreographer, Stage manager.

## Write a note for an actor

1. **Tap a word** in the line the note is about.
2. Press the notes department (**NOTE**).
3. Say who it is for. The field reads "For: blank =" and the name of whoever speaks the line:
   - leave it **blank** for that speaker;
   - press **Everyone**, or the names of other characters, to send it elsewhere.
4. Type the note in the field "Note for the cast". Enter.

A note on an action or a heading, with nothing in "For", goes to everyone.

The note belongs to the whole line, whichever word you tapped. It shows in your margin like a cue, is listed under **Review** → "Notes for the cast", and stays off the cue sheets.

During a rehearsal, pin notes as the director gives them. Afterwards you have them all, each on its line.

## Send the file

1. **☰** → **Send to cast…**. The box says how many notes wait and for whom. Check it against what you expect.
2. Press **Save the cast file (.html)**. It is named after the show, `…-cast.html`.
3. Send that one file to the cast the way you send everything else: the group chat, mail, a shared folder.
4. **Save a revision** named for it ("Cast file, 12 October"). The cast file does not record itself in the history; this is how you will know what the cast has.

What goes: the text without cues, comments or to-do notes, and the notes of the cast departments. What stays: everything else.

## What to tell the actors, the first time

> Open the file in your phone's browser. Press **My part** and tick every name you speak under, the groups you are in too. **Hide my lines** covers your lines so you can practise; tap one to see it. Tap any line to add a note of your own. When I send a new file, open it the same way: your notes stay.

## Send again after changes

Do exactly the same: **Send to cast…** → **Save the cast file (.html)**, and send it.

When an actor opens the new file it starts with **What is new**: the notes to read, the lines that changed and how many are theirs, and any of their own notes that sit on a changed line. Their part, their notes and what they had read are kept.

Notes you have dealt with: delete them in the book (tap the tag, **Delete**) before sending, and they are not in the next file. Notes you leave in are sent again, and stay marked as read for those who read them.

## Try it once with one person

Before the whole cast depends on it, send the file to one actor and ask them to: open it, tick their part, write a note, close everything, open the file again. If the part and the note are still there, their phone keeps them.

If not (some mail and chat apps open attachments in a viewer that remembers nothing), ask them to save the file to the phone and open it from the browser. This has not yet been tried on real phones; treat the first send as the trial.

## If something is off

- **An actor changed phone, or their notes are gone.** On the old device: the menu → **Export my notes**. On the new one: open the cast file, then open that export from the menu (**Import my notes…**).
- **"This file is older than the one you have."** They opened last week's file. Nothing was changed; they should open the newest.
- **An actor sees notes for others.** Under "All", yes: one file goes to everyone. Do not write in a cast note what only one person should read.
- **A note went to the wrong person.** A blank "For" follows the speaker of the line. If the note was dragged to another speaker's line, it is now theirs. Open it and name the character.
- **Someone already keeps the reader and wants only the show.** **Text and notes only (.cast.json)** is that: smaller, opened from the reader's menu.
