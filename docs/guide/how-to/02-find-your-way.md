# How to find your way around the book

Where things are on the screen, and the quickest way to get to a place in the script.

## Learn the buttons

Press **Guide** in the bottom right corner, or H. Every button on screen gets a number, and a card names each one, says what it does and which key does the same. Esc closes it. **?** lists every key.

## The screen

- **The spread.** The script page on the left: names, text, and the cue margin on the right of it. The facing page on the right holds the boards for that page.
- **The top bar**, left to right: Menu; pens, Eraser, Finger; Undo, Redo; the act and scene list; Find; the three view switches (Boards, Cues, Ink); Review, Cue list, Print, Show.
- **The corner**, bottom right: night mode, zoom, Guide, the key list.
- **Beside the title:** whether the show is saved.

## Get to a place

| To go to | Do |
|---|---|
| An act or a scene | The **Jump to…** list in the top bar. Each entry shows its page. |
| A word or a phrase | The magnifier, or Ctrl+F, or `/`. Type, Enter for the next, Shift+Enter for the one before. Capitals and accents do not matter. Esc leaves. |
| The next or previous page | Page Down, Page Up |
| The top or the end | Home, End |
| A particular cue | **Cue list** (L), then press its row |
| Something the tool is unsure about | **Review** (R), then press the entry |

The browser's own find (Ctrl+F in other pages) would miss most of the script, because only the pages near the screen are drawn. That is why the tool has its own, on the same key.

## Change what you see

| To | Do |
|---|---|
| Read the text alone, as the author would | Switch **Cues** off (C) and **Ink** off (I) |
| Give the script the whole width | Switch **Boards** off (B) |
| Work in a dark room | Night mode: the moon in the corner, or N. Print stays light. |
| Make the page larger or smaller | **+** and **−** in the corner or on the keyboard; **Fit** (0) fills the width |

Zoom makes the page larger; it never changes where a line breaks. What you see is what prints.

These choices belong to the machine, not to the show: they are remembered in this browser and do not travel with the file.

## Touch, pen and mouse

Each does one thing, with no mode to switch.

| | On the script |
|---|---|
| Pen tip | Writes |
| Pen eraser end, or its button | Rubs out |
| Finger tap on a word | Opens the box to pin a cue |
| Finger drag | Scrolls |
| Double tap, or a long press, on a speech | Opens its text to edit |
| Mouse | Does what a finger does |

No pen on this machine? Press **Finger** (F): finger and mouse draw. Press it again to get tapping and scrolling back.

The single-letter keys work when you are not typing in a field and no cue box is open.

## When you cannot find a button

- **Review has no number.** Then nothing needs a look. The panel still opens, with the characters and what is commented out.
- **The top bar is gone.** You are in show mode. Press **Leave** twice, or S twice.
- **The toolbar is in two rows.** The window is narrower than about 1280 px. Everything is still there.
