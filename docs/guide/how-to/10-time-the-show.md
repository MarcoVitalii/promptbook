# How to time the show in rehearsal

Teach the book how long each part of the show really takes, so that on the night the line follows the stage and the countdowns to the cues can be believed.

## Before you start

- A run of the show, or of an act, without stops if possible.
- The cues pinned. They are not needed for timing, but then you see the calls working as you go.
- Decide how fine you want it. **From time to time** is the way a run starts: the line runs and you double-tap a word now and then, once or twice a page. **Line by line** is exact: one press at the end of every speech. Use it for a tricky sequence.

## Steps

1. Scroll to where the run starts.
2. Press **Show** (or S). The book is now read only and the panel is on the right.
3. Press **Time it** in the panel. It lights. (It is off every time you enter show mode; you must turn it on.) Every speech now shows its time at the right edge of the page: `~` in front means it is only inferred from the number of words.
4. **Double-tap the first word** that will be spoken. The line goes there.
5. As the actor starts, press **Space**. The line runs.
6. **As the show goes on, double-tap the word being spoken.** Each tap says "the show is here": the real time since your last tap is shared among the speeches in between, by their length. The line may have run ahead of the actors or fallen behind them; tap where they are. Arrow down does the same for "the next speech has begun", if your hands are on the keyboard.
   After each one the panel says what was timed: its time now, what it was before, and how many blocks. The panel also counts the time since your last tap.
7. **When the run stops** (a note from the director, a repeat), press **Space** at once. Time with the line stopped is not counted.
8. **To pick up elsewhere:** with the line stopped, double-tap the new place, then Space when they start.
9. At the end, press Space, then **Leave** twice (or S twice).

The times are saved with the show.

## Line by line, for exact times

1. With **Time it** on, press **Line by line** in the panel. The line goes to the start of the speech it is in, and a **Next** button appears.
2. As the actor starts, press **Space**. The line does not run: it waits on the speech while the panel counts.
3. When the speech is over, press **Next** (or Arrow down). The speech gets exactly that time and the line waits on the next one.
4. Go on, one press for each speech. To skip ahead, double-tap a later speech: the ones you skipped share the time.
5. **From time to time** takes you back to the running line.

While the line waits, the calls do not count down. A rough run later leaves these times in proportion: it does not flatten them.

## A stretch with no words

A fight, a dance, a scene change: the script says "(They fight.)" and the stage takes two minutes.

Either mark the word before it and the word after it, with the line running: the action in between gets the time.

Or type it: with **Time it** on, press the time of that block at the right edge of the page, type `120` or `2:00`, and press Enter. It then shows with `=` in front, and later timings around it leave it alone. An empty box and Enter forget it. **+5 s** and **−5 s** in the panel do the same for the block the line is in.

## If you tapped wrong

Press **Undo** in the panel straight after. It takes back the last timing.

A tap far off (while the line is stopped, backwards, or giving a time that makes no sense) is not kept as a time anyway. It only moves the line, and the panel says why nothing was timed.

## How much is done

With **Time it** on, the panel shows the share of the show that is timed. You do not need 100%: what is not timed runs at the pace of the show, 130 words a minute to begin with.

Once enough speech is timed, the panel offers the pace it measured ("Rest at … wpm"). Press it and everything not yet timed uses your company's real pace instead of the default. You can also set the number in **Settings** ("Pace of blocks not timed").

## Afterwards

- **Save a revision** ("Timed, run of 20 October"). The times are part of the show and come back with a restore.
- A later rewrite forgets the time of the speeches it changes, and only of those. Time that scene again at the next run.
- To start over: **Settings** → **Forget … timed blocks**.

## If something is off

- **Nothing is being timed.** **Time it** is not lit, or the clock is not running (Space). Both are needed. The panel says so after a tap that timed nothing.
- **The line ran off while I was not looking.** Stop it, double-tap where the show is, start again. The stretch you missed keeps the times it had.
- **The line is always ahead (or behind).** That is what timing fixes. If it is still so after a timed run, on that night use the pace control, not Time it ([Call a show](11-call-a-show.md)).
