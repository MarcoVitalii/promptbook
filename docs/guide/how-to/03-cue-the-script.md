# How to put the cues in

Pin cues on their words, close the ones that end, and get a cue sheet for each operator.

## Pin a cue on a word

1. **Tap the word** the cue is called on.
2. **Press the department** in the box that opens (LX, SND, …). With a keyboard, the first letter of its code does the same.
3. The cue is there, with the next number of that department already given. **Type the margin text** (what shows on the tag: "blackout") and press Enter. Tab takes you to the description, a longer text that appears only in the cue list. To give another number, Shift+Tab goes back to it.

The tag sits in the margin with a line to its word, and the word is boxed in the department's colour.

To put a cue **between two speeches** (on a silence, a scene change), tap the space between them: the box is headed "Cue on its own line, after this block". Or tap the last word of the speech above and tick **On its own line** before pressing the department.

## Change, move or delete one

- **Change:** tap its tag. The same box opens.
- **Move:** drag the tag onto another word, or to the space between two speeches. With a finger, hold the tag for a moment first, until it lifts. It can be carried to another page: the script scrolls at the edges.
- **Delete:** tap the tag, then **Delete**; or the Delete key while the box is open and the cursor is in no field.

Each is one step of Undo.

## Close a cue that runs

A sound that plays until it is faded, a state held until restored: pin the start as usual. Then, where it ends:

1. **Tap the word** where it stops.
2. In the box, the row **End:** lists the cues still open at that point. **Press the one that ends here.**

The end has no number of its own. Between start and end a thin line runs down the margin of every page, so you can see what is running wherever you open the book.

When one cue *replaces* another (sound 6 comes in and takes out sound 5), make the end as above, open it and press **Make it a cue**: it becomes a numbered cue of its own that also ends the other.

## About numbers

The tool offers the next number and never changes one afterwards. A cue pinned between 5 and 6 is offered 6 again; call it 5.5, or leave it and renumber later, as you would on paper. The cue list marks a number used twice, and one out of order with ↕. They are hints: nothing stops you printing.

To renumber a cue, change its number in its box. Its end follows.

## Leave yourself a note

Pin under **TODO** instead of a department: "ask about this entrance". It shows in the margin like a cue, is listed at the top of **Review**, and never appears on a cue sheet. Tick it in Review when it is dealt with and it is gone.

## Get the cue sheets

Press **Cue list** (L).

| For | Do |
|---|---|
| One department on screen | The filter at the top of the list |
| A sheet per operator, on paper | **Print by dept** |
| A spreadsheet | **CSV**; **By dept (.zip)** for one file per department |

Each row has the number, the margin text, the act and scene, the page and the words around the cue. The list is read from the book each time: do not keep an old export, make a new one.

## Check before you hand them out

1. **Review** (R): "Ends with no cue to close" and "Unknown departments" should be empty.
2. **Cue list:** look for numbers in bold (used twice) and for ↕.
3. Switch **Cues** off and on (C): a quick way to see whether a page that should be busy is.

## If something is off

- **Tapping a word draws a dot.** **Finger** is on. Press it (F).
- **A cue is grey.** Its department is not in Settings: the code was changed after the cue was pinned. Add the code back, or open the cue and pin it under the new one.
- **The end I need is not offered.** Only cues an end can reach are listed: the latest open one of each department, and earlier ones with a different number. Give the two cues different numbers first.
