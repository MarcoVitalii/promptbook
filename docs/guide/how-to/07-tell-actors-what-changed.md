# How to tell each actor what changed for them

After a rewrite, give every actor a short list of the changes that touch their part, and only those.

## Before you start

You need a revision from before the change to compare with. If you saved one at the last rehearsal, you have it. (If not, there is nothing to compare: save one today, for next time.)

## Steps

1. **☰** → **History…**
2. Press **Changes** beside the revision the cast last worked from. The box compares that revision with the show as it is now.
   To compare two older revisions instead, choose them under **From** and **To**.
3. Read the list. It is grouped by act and scene, with the page:
   - words cut are struck through in red;
   - words added are underlined in green;
   - each entry says whether the speech was added, cut or changed.
4. Open **Show** and choose who the list is for:

   | Choose | To get |
   |---|---|
   | **Text** | Every change to the words, without cue changes. For the director, or to read at the start of rehearsal. |
   | A character's name | What reaches that actor. The number beside each name says how many changes; a character with none has nothing to learn again. |
   | **Everything, cues too** | Text and cue changes. For yourself and the operators. |

5. Press **Print** to print the list as shown. One character at a time gives one sheet per actor.

## What "reaches" an actor

For a character, the list holds:

- changes in the speeches they speak, alone or with others;
- changes in an action that names them in capitals;
- changes in the speech just before one of theirs, marked "the line before yours", because that is their cue to speak.

It leaves out cue changes and anything commented out.

It is a reading of the text, not of the staging. An actor who is on stage and silent through a rewritten scene is not told, and neither is one covered only by "ALL BUT MARCO". For a scene that changed a great deal, say so to the whole company and give everyone the **Text** list for that scene.

## Send it another way

- **On paper:** the printed sheet, with the changed pages ([Reissue changed pages](06-reissue-changed-pages.md)).
- **On the phone:** a new cast file shows each actor "What is new" by itself when they open it ([Send to the cast](09-send-to-the-cast.md)). The two do not exclude each other.

## If something is off

- **A character is missing from "Show".** The list offers the names in `characters` at the top of the script; without that list, every name that speaks alone. Add the character there.
- **A whole speech shows as cut and another as added,** when it was only reworded. More than half its words changed, so the tool no longer takes them for the same speech. The information is right, only less pretty.
- **A moved speech shows as cut in one place and added in another.** That is what a move is, to someone holding the old pages.
- **"No changes."** You are comparing a revision with itself, or the changes were to cues only and **Text** is chosen.
