# How to reissue only the pages that changed

The cast has paper. The text has changed. You want to print the sheets that are now wrong, with the numbers everyone already has, and nothing else.

## The day you hand out paper

Lock the page numbers at the moment you print. Do this once, before the first sheets go out.

1. **☰** → **History…**
2. Name the revision: "Printed for the cast, 12 October".
3. Tick **Lock page numbers here**.
4. Press **Save revision**.
5. Print: **Print** in the top bar → **Script pages only** for the actors, or **Book: script and facing pages** for yourself.

The History box now says "Page numbers are locked at …". Nothing looks different on the pages. From here on, a page starts where it starts on that paper.

## After a change

Take in the rewrite as usual ([how](04-take-in-a-rewrite.md)). Then:

1. **☰** → **History…**. Under the lock it lists **Changed pages** and **Pages now empty**.
2. Press **Print changed pages**. Only those script pages print, with no blank sheets.
3. Hand them out with the list. Each printed sheet carries "∗ changed since “Printed for the cast, 12 October”" at its foot, so a sheet in someone's folder can be told from the one it replaces.

What the cast does with them:

- **A page with the same number** (14) replaces the old 14.
- **A lettered page** (14A, 14B) is new and goes after 14.
- **A page listed as now empty** comes out of the folder. Its number is not used again.

Their handwritten notes on a replaced sheet are theirs to copy across.

## When the changed pages pile up

After several rounds, half the book is marked and there are pages 14A to 14F. Time to print everything again and start clean.

1. **☰** → **History…**, name the revision, tick **Lock page numbers here**, **Save revision**. This revision is now the paper.
2. Print the whole script and collect the old copies.

Lettered pages keep their letters when you lock again. To go back to plain 1, 2, 3 numbering, press **Unlock** before you save and lock: every page is then renumbered, so do it only when *all* the paper is being replaced.

## Good to know

- **A cue change does not mark a page.** Only words do. The actors' pages do not need reprinting because a light cue moved.
- **Your own book follows the same numbers.** The cue list, Review and the scene list show 14A where the page says 14A, so "from the top of 14A" means one place for everybody.
- **A lock belongs to its variant.** The touring version has its own paper and its own lock.
- **Who changed, not only where.** To tell each actor which of their lines changed, see [Tell each actor what changed](07-tell-actors-what-changed.md).

## If something is off

- **Every page is marked as changed.** The paper size was changed in Settings after locking, or a large block was added at the very top. Check Settings → Paper. If the paper really is being replaced whole, lock again.
- **A page is marked and I see no difference.** A speech before it grew by a line and pushed the break. The words are the same but they no longer sit where they sat on paper, so the sheet is wrong all the same.
- **No "Print changed pages".** Page numbers are not locked. The box says "Page numbers are free".
